I am a social scientist working at the intersection of political economy and public policy. My focus is on producing actionable research to inform governments and institutions, especially on inequality, welfare, and socioeconomic policy.

I have worked in both academia and the public sector, including roles at the Generalitat de Catalunya, the Spanish government, and the European Commission.

I hold a Ph.D. in Social and Political Sciences from the European University Institute, with a background in economics and international relations. My research has been published in leading outlets such as the *Journal of European Social Policy*, *European Journal of Political Research*, and by *Cambridge University Press*.
